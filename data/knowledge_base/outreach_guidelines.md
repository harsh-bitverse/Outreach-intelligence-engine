# Research Outreach Communication & Integrity Guidelines

## 1. Core Principles & Permission Boundaries

- **Local-First & Quality-Gated**: Never spam or batch-dispatch indiscriminately.
- **Strict Human-Approval Boundary**: The AI agent **CANNOT** autonomously send emails, replies, or follow-ups. Every reply and follow-up draft requires distinct, manual human review and explicit operator triggering.
- **Permanent Suppression Safeguards**: Any reply, rejection, bounce, opt-out, or self-institutional domain (`@iitd.ac.in`) must instantly halt and permanently suppress all outreach to that recipient.
- **Truthful & Substantiated**: Every claim, project achievement, metric, and research connection must be strictly backed by the verified student knowledge base. Never invent open lab positions, funding commitments, or non-existent papers.

---

## 2. Four-Paragraph Structure (130-180 Words)

Every initial outreach email must conform to the user's four-paragraph structure:

1. **Paragraph 1: Identity & Clear Purpose (25-35 words)**
   - State identity: Harshith N, undergraduate in Chemical Engineering at IIT Delhi.
   - Purpose: Inquiring about prospective research internship opportunities for Summer 2027 in the professor's group.

2. **Paragraph 2: Faculty Research Relevance (35-50 words)**
   - Reference the professor's specific research area, lab focus, or recent papers from the verified database.
   - Articulate genuine intellectual interest in their specific methodologies or problem statements.

3. **Paragraph 3: Substantive Student Project Bridge (45-65 words)**
   - Draw a direct, concrete connection to one of Harshith's substantiated projects:
     - *SciML/PDEs*: Neural Process Twin (surrogate models for thermal fields, physical constraints).
     - *Systems/AI*: LLM Inference Intelligence Engine (hardware discovery, roofline bottleneck profiling, latency benchmarks).
     - *Numerical Methods*: PINN error estimation benchmark (Krasowski et al. reproduction, soft boundary conditions).
     - *Thermodynamics/Simulation*: Modern C++17 Peng-Robinson EOS solver.
     - *Robotics/Control*: Embedded servo-actuated humanoid robotics.
   - Highlight practical software, modeling, or experimental skills.

4. **Paragraph 4: Summer 2027 Request & Polite Close (25-35 words)**
   - Express eagerness to contribute for 10-12 weeks in Summer 2027.
   - Propose a brief conversation or exchange to discuss potential alignment.
   - State that CV is attached for their consideration.

---

## 3. Strict Signature Specification

The signature must **NEVER** contain LinkedIn, GitHub, or extraneous links. It must strictly follow:

```text
Sincerely,
Harshith N
Undergraduate, Department of Chemical Engineering
Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi)
ch1251635@iitd.ac.in
```

---

## 4. Tone Guidelines

- **Intellectually curious and professional**: Focus on scientific problem-solving.
- **Concise and direct**: Respect faculty time; stay strictly within 130-180 words.
- **No generic flattery**: Avoid vague superlatives ("I am in awe of your groundbreaking work"). Address the specific scientific work directly.
